Alice in Borderland: Quick Summary 
Format: Japanese live-action Netflix series (not a movie).

Genre: Survival Thriller / Sci-Fi Drama.

Plot: People are trapped in a deserted, parallel version of Tokyo (the Borderland) and must compete in deadly, sadistic games to survive and extend their "visa."

The Games:

Suits = Game Type: Spades (Physical), Clubs (Teamwork), Diamonds (Intellect), Hearts (Psychology/Betrayal).

Main Characters:

Arisu (The "Alice"): A genius gamer and main protagonist, specializing in strategy.
